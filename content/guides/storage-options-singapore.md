If your move-out and move-in dates don't line up, you generally have two
paths: rent a self-storage unit and move items yourself, or use
move-linked storage where a mover picks up, stores, and redelivers for
you.

## Self-Storage

You rent a unit directly and handle the trip yourself, or hire a one-off
job to move things in. This suits people who need to access their items
regularly, since you keep a key.

## Move-Linked Storage

We pick up, pack and protect items, hold them, then redeliver when
you're ready. This suits renovation gaps, handover mismatches, or staged
office moves, since it's one coordinated job instead of two.

- **When it fits**: renovation still running after move-out, handover
  dates that don't match, staged office relocation, decluttering before
  a sale
- **Pricing**: SGD 60 per cubic metre per month, based on volume and
  duration
- **Access**: flag upfront if you'll need to retrieve items mid-storage,
  since access needs advance notice

## Which One to Pick

If you need frequent access and don't mind handling transport yourself,
self-storage is more flexible. If you'd rather have pickup, storage and
redelivery handled as one job with no separate transport to arrange,
move-linked storage is usually less hassle for a one-off gap.
