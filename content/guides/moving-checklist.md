A short list of what actually prevents a move from going wrong, gathered
from the questions that come up most.

## Before You Book

- Get a full written quote covering manpower, transport, stairs and
  materials, not just a "from $X" headline price
- Ask if packing, dismantling and reassembly are included or extra
- Check reviews for mentions of surprise charges or unclear
  communication
- Confirm your building's moving hour rules, especially for condos (see
  our [condo moving guide](/guides/condo-moving))
- If your dates don't line up, ask about [storage](/storage) rather than
  booking two separate jobs

## Common Ways Moves Go Wrong

- **Unrealistically low quotes** that add stairs, manpower or bulky-item
  charges afterward
- **Poor handling** from untrained or unlicensed crews
- **No clear communication**, leading to late arrivals or confused
  scope on the day

See our [guide to hidden fees](/guides/moving-costs-hidden-fees) for the
specific line items to watch for.

## Timing Your Move

- Weekends and month-end dates see higher demand; book early if your
  date isn't flexible
- If you're between a resale flat's offer and completion, or waiting on
  a BTO key collection, book your mover once dates are confirmed rather
  than provisionally, since availability tightens close to popular dates
- Downsizing or decluttering before a move takes longer than expected;
  start sorting before you get a quote, not after

## On Moving Day

- Confirm access: lift booking, loading bay, parking, and any permit or
  deposit receipt
- Walk the crew through what's fragile, what's being dismantled, and
  what's not going to the new address
- Keep essentials (documents, chargers, medication) with you rather than
  in the truck
