An ad for "$288 per truck" sounds like a deal. Often it's a starting
price with everything that actually moves your furniture charged as an
add-on afterward.

## What Gets Left Out of Lowball Quotes

- **Manpower.** Only the truck is included; loading and unloading cost
  extra.
- **Stairs or no lift access.** An additional SGD 10-30 per floor is
  common.
- **Dismantling and reassembly.** Beds, cabinets and tables are often not
  included.
- **Packing materials.** Boxes, stretch film and bubble wrap billed
  separately.
- **Waiting time.** Delays get charged per 30 minutes.

## How This Adds Up

A "$288 truck" booking can turn into: SGD 60 for manpower, SGD 80 for
dismantling, SGD 40 for bubble wrap, SGD 100 for a long carry, and
SGD 100 for a two-floor staircase surcharge. Total: SGD 668, more than
double the advertised rate.

## Questions to Ask Before Booking Any Mover

1. Is manpower included in the quote?
2. Are there staircase or lift-access surcharges?
3. Will I be charged for packing materials?
4. What's the cost for dismantling and reassembly?
5. Is there a charge for long carry distances or waiting time?

Get a full written quotation with these items itemised, not a single
"from $X" number.

## What to Look For in Reviews

Scan for phrases like "surprise charges", "didn't mention", or "extra
fees on moving day". These are signs a quote isn't the final price.
