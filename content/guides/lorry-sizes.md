Book a lorry too small and you're paying for a second trip. Book one too
big and you're paying for space you never use. Here's how to tell which
you need.

## The Two Common Sizes

- **10ft lorry**: roughly 10ft long, 5.5ft wide, 6ft high
- **14ft lorry**: roughly 14ft long, 6.5ft wide, 6.5ft high

The extra width and height matter more than the length alone: they add
real stacking space, not just a longer bed.

## What Fits a 10ft Lorry

Studio apartments, 1-room or 2-room HDB flats, or a partial move:

- 15 to 20 medium boxes
- A queen or single bed frame and mattress
- A 2-seater sofa
- A study table and chair
- A small wardrobe or shelving unit
- Small appliances (microwave, fan, small TV)

## What Fits a 14ft Lorry

3-room to 4-room HDB flats, or a condo unit with a fuller set of
furniture:

- 30 to 40 medium boxes
- A queen or king bed frame, mattress and headboard
- A 3-seater sofa or sofa set
- A dining table with 4-6 chairs
- A wardrobe or two, plus shelving
- Major appliances: washing machine, fridge, TV console

## Quick Rule of Thumb

- Under 20 boxes plus 2 large furniture pieces: 10ft lorry likely works
- 20 to 40 boxes plus several large pieces: go with 14ft
- More than that, or bulky items like a piano or multiple wardrobes: you
  may need two trips, or a bigger lorry

Boxes stack more efficiently than furniture, so a move with fewer but
bulkier items may need a bigger lorry than the box count alone suggests.
