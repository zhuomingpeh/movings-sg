Condo moves have a layer HDB moves don't: your MCST's rules on lift
booking, permitted hours, and moving permits. Sort these before you book
a crew, not on moving day.

## Before You Book

- **Service lift booking.** Most condos require this in advance, and
  slots fill up fast around weekends and month-end.
- **Permitted moving hours.** Many restrict moving after 6-9pm; check
  before assuming a [midnight move](/midnight-movers) is an option.
- **Moving permit or deposit.** Some MCSTs require a refundable deposit
  or a permit application submitted days ahead.
- **Floor and lift protection.** Some buildings require this to be
  arranged (and sometimes paid for) before movers arrive.
- **Loading bay access.** Check if there's a marked bay, and how far the
  carry is from there to the lift.

## On Moving Day

- Confirm your booked lift slot with the management office that morning
- Have your permit or deposit receipt on hand in case security asks
- Tell your crew about any protection requirements before they start
  carrying

## What This Affects

Loading bay distance and lift booking windows affect how long a move
takes and how much manpower is worth booking. See the
[full rate table](/pricing) for how access factors into cost, or
[house moving](/house-moving) for the wider HDB and condo moving scope.
