Some items need more than a blanket and a trolley. We handle artwork,
antiques, pianos, safes and server equipment with the crating and
technique each one calls for.

## Artwork and Antiques

- **Paintings and sculptures**: packed and transported from delicate
  canvases to large sculptures
- **Antique furniture**: handled with techniques suited to fragile,
  valuable pieces
- **Collectibles and heirlooms**: moved with care

Custom crates are built where needed, using acid-free paper, bubble
wrap and padded blankets for full protection in transit.

## Pianos

Pricing runs SGD 120-500 depending on piano type, floor level, access
and manpower needed. See the [rate table](/pricing) for how this fits a
full move quote.

## Safes, Gym Equipment and Servers

Safe boxes (from SGD 60++), gym equipment (from SGD 100++), and server
or IT equipment are quoted by weight, route and dismantling needs. Server
shutdown and reconnection should be coordinated by your own IT team; we
handle physical protection and transport. See
[office moving](/office-moving) for the wider commercial IT-handling
scope.
