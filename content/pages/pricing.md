## What Pushes the Price Up

- **No lift access or stairs.** Stair carry needs more manpower and time.
  See our [no-lift-access page](/no-lift-access-movers) if this applies to you.
- **Long carry to the loading bay.** Condo basement routes and restricted
  loading bays add labour.
- **Dismantling and reassembly.** Beds, wardrobes, workstations and large
  cabinets need tools and setup time.
- **Weekend or after-hours moves.** Common for offices working around MCST
  rules, subject to availability and a timing surcharge.
- **Urgent booking.** Same-day or last-minute moves can cost more when we
  need to pull in extra manpower or a vehicle slot.
- **Heavy or fragile items.** Pianos, safes, gym equipment, fish tanks and
  medical beds need extra handling.

## What's Included as Standard

- Moving truck and manpower for normal loading and unloading
- Boxes and packing materials for suitable moves
- Shrink wrap and carton protection
- Basic coordination for HDB, condo, landed and commercial access rules
- A clear quote confirmed before the move starts

## What Costs Extra

Packing and unpacking, dismantling and assembly, disposal, storage,
special equipment handling, stair carry, long carry, extra manpower,
after-hours or weekend timing, urgent booking, and building protection
requirements (some MCSTs require lift or corridor padding). These are
quoted only when they apply to your move.

## FAQ

**How much do movers cost in Singapore?**
Basic moves start from SGD 200-450 for a 1-room HDB flat, up to
SGD 1,000++ for a landed property. See the table above for your flat size.

**What's included in the standard price?**
The moving truck, manpower for normal loading and unloading, boxes,
packing materials and shrink wrap.

**Do you charge extra for stairs or no lift access?**
Yes. Extra manpower for stairs-only moves is SGD 60-120, depending on
access and scope. Tell us in advance so we assign the right crew size.

**Can storage be included in a moving quote?**
Yes, at SGD 60 per cubic metre per month. See [storage](/storage) for
details.

**How do I get a fixed quote?**
Send photos or a video walkthrough, your item list, pickup and delivery
addresses, floor level, lift access, and your preferred moving date on
