If your move-out date, renovation, handover or office setup doesn't line
up with your move-in date, we can hold your items in storage and handle
pickup, packing, storage and redelivery as one plan instead of three
separate bookings.

## Storage Types

- **Short-term storage** for gaps of a few days or weeks during
  renovation, key handover, or a delayed move-in
- **Long-term storage** for furniture, cartons, appliances or files kept
  for months or longer
- **Residential storage** for HDB, condo, landed, rental handover,
  decluttering, or overseas relocation
- **Office and commercial storage** for workstations, cabinets, documents
  and IT equipment during a phased office move
- **Move-linked storage**: pickup from the old address, storage in
  between, redelivery to the new one

## How Storage Pricing Works

Storage is **SGD 60 per cubic metre per month**. The exact quote depends
on:

| Factor | What affects the quote |
|---|---|
| Item volume | Number of cartons, furniture size, whether items can be stacked safely |
| Duration | Short-term gap, renovation, staged office move, or long-term storage |
| Packing and protection | Cartons, shrink wrap, fragile-item wrapping, dismantling before storage |
| Pickup and redelivery access | Lift access, stairs, loading bay distance, MCST rules and timing |
| Add-on work | Disposal, dismantling, IT equipment handling, after-hours or weekend move |

## Minimum Duration and Access

Minimum storage duration depends on your move schedule, not a fixed
facility minimum, so tell us whether you need days, weeks or months.
If you'll need to retrieve items while they're in storage, flag this
before everything is packed. Access and retrieval need advance notice.

## What Can Be Stored

- Furniture, beds, sofas, dining sets, cabinets, loose household items
- Cartons, clothes, personal belongings, books, small appliances
- Office furniture, workstations, chairs, files, display items, stock and
  non-hazardous equipment
- Anything properly packed, dry, clean and safe for storage

## What Cannot Be Stored

- Food, perishables, waste, damp items, leaking liquids, or items with
  pest issues
- Hazardous, flammable, explosive, toxic or illegal materials
- Cash, jewellery, passports, original legal documents, or other
  irreplaceable valuables
- Live plants or animals, and anything needing temperature-controlled
  handling unless we've confirmed that separately

## How Storage Combines With a Move

1. **Survey and quote.** Send photos, an item list, access details,
   estimated duration, pickup address and delivery address if known.
2. **Packing and labelling.** Cartons, furniture and office items are
   packed or protected before pickup where needed.
3. **Pickup and transport.** Our team collects from the home, office or
   commercial unit.
4. **Storage placement.** Items go into storage based on volume and
   handling needs.
5. **Redelivery.** When the new home or office is ready, items go back
   out and get placed according to your plan.

## Good Times to Use Temporary Storage

- Renovation work is still ongoing after you've moved out
- Handover and key collection dates don't match
- You're downsizing and need time to decide what to keep
- An office move has to happen in phases to reduce downtime
- You need to declutter before selling, renting or handing over a unit
- Overseas relocation or a new office setup creates a timing gap

## FAQ

**How does storage pricing work?**
It depends on item volume, storage duration, packing needs, pickup
access, and whether dismantling or disposal is included. Base rate is
SGD 60 per cubic metre per month.

**Is there a minimum storage duration?**
It depends on your move schedule rather than a fixed facility policy.
Share your expected storage period before booking.

**Can I access my items during storage?**
Depends on the arrangement. Mention it upfront if you expect to retrieve
items while they're stored.

**What can't be stored?**
Perishables, hazardous or flammable materials, illegal goods, live
plants or animals, and cash, jewellery, passports or other irreplaceable
documents.

**Can storage be combined with a move?**
Yes. Packing, pickup, storage and redelivery can be quoted and scheduled
as one job. See our [pricing page](/pricing) for the full rate table.
