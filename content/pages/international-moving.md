Relocating out of Singapore involves more coordination than a local
move: packing for freight, documentation, and timing your local move-out
against a shipping or flight schedule. We handle the Singapore side of
that: packing, crating fragile items, and getting your shipment to the
freight forwarder or agent you're using.

## What We Coordinate

- Packing and crating for sea or air freight
- Furniture dismantling before crating
- Temporary storage if your move-out date is ahead of your shipping
  window
- Disposal of items you're not taking with you

## Plan This Alongside Storage

International moves rarely line up neatly: flights get booked before
shipping dates are confirmed, or a shipping slot falls through. If
there's a gap, see [storage](/storage) so your move-out doesn't have to
wait on your move-out-of-the-country date.

## Get a Quote

Send us your destination country, moving date, and an item list or
photos, and we'll scope the Singapore side of the move: packing, local
transport to your freight forwarder, and any storage in between.
