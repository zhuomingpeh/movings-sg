Most moves turn up furniture, appliances or office items that shouldn't
go to the new address. We clear these as part of your move so you're not
paying to transport things you don't want anymore.

## What We Clear

- **Bulky items**: large furniture, mattresses, wardrobes, sofas
- **Renovation debris**: construction waste, wood, tiles, drywall
- **Appliances**: fridges, washing machines, ovens, electronics
- **Office and commercial**: old furniture, equipment, unwanted inventory

## How It Works

1. **Assessment.** Tell us what needs disposing of; we evaluate size,
   volume and any dismantling needed, then give an upfront quote.
2. **Collection.** On your scheduled date, we remove the items and
   transport them to an approved facility.
3. **Disposal.** Items are processed following Singapore's disposal
   regulations, with recycling used where possible.

## Cost

Disposal runs SGD 80-200 depending on volume and item type. See the
[full rate table](/pricing) for how this combines with a moving quote.

## Scope

This page covers disposal that's part of a move: clearing items before
packing, or after a renovation, alongside your moving or storage job. We
don't run item-level disposal-only pages here (sofa-only, mattress-only,
and so on); for a move-related job, send us photos, the pickup address,
lift or stair access, and your preferred date.
