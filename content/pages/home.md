Moving Solutions moves homes and offices across Singapore: HDB flats,
condos, landed property and commercial units, with packing, storage and
disposal handled by the same crew instead of three different vendors.

## What a Move Costs

Basic moves start from **SGD 200-450** for a 1-room HDB flat and scale up
by flat size and access. See the full [rate table](/pricing) for every
flat size, add-on service, and what changes the price before you book.

## Our Services

- **[House moving](/house-moving)**: HDB, condo and landed, with lift
  access, MCST rules, and stair carry planned for upfront
- **[Office moving](/office-moving)**: weekday or after-hours relocation,
  F&B equipment, printers, and secure/safe moving
- **[Packing](/packing)**: packing and unpacking, furniture dismantling
  and assembly
- **[Storage](/storage)**: short and long-term storage from SGD 60 per
  cubic metre per month, linked to your move dates
- **[Disposal](/disposal)**: unwanted furniture and bulky items cleared
  as part of a move
