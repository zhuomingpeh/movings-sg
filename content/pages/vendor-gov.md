Moving Solutions supports vendors and government offices with moving
services that follow procurement rules and strict timelines.

## What's Included

- Packing and transport of office or project equipment
- Coordination with government agencies or vendor teams
- Inventory management and careful handling
- Optional storage and disposal

## What to Expect

Full compliance with the buyer's process, clear coordination, and
minimal disruption to operations. Share your quotation format,
documentation requirements, scope, moving window and site access rules
early so the job can be planned properly.

## Our Process

1. **Consultation and survey.** Assess project needs, regulations and
   timeline.
2. **Packing and handling.** Items packed securely, heavy equipment
   managed safely.
3. **Transport and delivery.** On-time delivery with proper
   documentation.
4. **Setup and unpacking.** Placement and assembly as required.
5. **Optional storage and disposal.** Flexible solutions for temporary
   storage or item removal.
