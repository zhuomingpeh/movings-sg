Shifting just a few blocks away, or to another unit in the same condo or
HDB block? You don't need a lorry for that, just a team to lift, carry
and shift safely.

## Good For

- Moving to a nearby block in the same HDB estate
- Shifting to another level within your condo or apartment
- Relocating just across the road
- Moving items between units in the same building
- Renovating and temporarily relocating to a nearby unit

## What's Included

- Uniformed, trained movers
- Lifting, carrying and shifting of bulky and heavy items
- Basic tools for dismantling and reassembly (beds, wardrobes, tables)
- Protective wraps and trolleys on request

## Why Manpower-Only

- **No vehicle needed.** Save on costs for short-distance moves.
- **Flexible booking.** Pay only for the hours you need.
- **Experienced with HDBs, condos, walk-ups and lifts.**
- **Same-day available**, subject to schedule.

## How to Book

Message us with pickup and drop-off addresses, number of movers needed,
item types, and whether stairs or lift access is involved. We reply
within 30 minutes.
