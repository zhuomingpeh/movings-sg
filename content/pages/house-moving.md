Moving into or out of a home comes with its own set of access rules,
whichever type of property you're in. We plan around lift booking,
corridor space, MCST rules and building timings rather than turning up
and figuring it out on the day.

## HDB Moves

HDB moves need planning around lift access, corridor space and permitted
moving hours. We handle:

- Careful packing and protection for fragile items and electronics
- Loading and transport through narrow corridors, lifts and staircases
- Dismantling and reassembly of bed frames, wardrobes and tables
- Coordination so the move stays inside your block's permitted hours

## Condo Moves

Condos add a layer that HDB doesn't: MCST rules, service lift booking,
and loading bay timing. Before moving day we confirm:

- Service lift slot and loading bay access
- Any move-in/move-out permit or deposit your MCST requires
- Floor and lift protection requirements
- Weekday vs weekend and after-hours rules, since many condos restrict
  moving past 6-9pm

## Landed Property Moves

Landed homes are generally more flexible on timing and access than condos,
but often involve more volume and multiple levels. We plan crew size
around floor count and stair carry if there's no lift.

## Our Process

1. **Move planning.** Share unit type, item list and preferred schedule.
2. **Packing and preparation.** Items packed and wrapped before moving day.
3. **Moving day execution.** Loading, transport and unloading, kept inside
   your building's rules and hours.
4. **Setup at your new home.** Furniture reassembled and placed where you
   want it.

Yes, our crews are experienced with HDB and older condo layouts.

Tell us your MCST's rules in advance and we'll plan around them, or

**Can you dismantle and reassemble furniture?**
