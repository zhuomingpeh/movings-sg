Moving Solutions was founded by two longtime friends with years of
hands-on experience in residential, commercial and specialty item
relocations across Singapore.

## What We Focus On

- **Clear communication.** From the first message to final delivery, we
  keep you informed rather than leaving you guessing.
- **Straightforward pricing.** Transparent quotes so you know what
  you're paying for before moving day.
- **One team, one job.** Packing, moving, disposal and storage under one
  booking instead of coordinating multiple vendors.

## Trusted By

We've moved offices for Sunjoy Group, VLocker, STATS ChipPAC,
Trelleborg, Sevenjoy, Arte by Dr M, Kroll, ADM, OOM Institute, DD, and
MCAS Darul Arqam, alongside residential moves across HDB, condo and
landed homes.
