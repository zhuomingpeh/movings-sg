## What to Check Before You Book Any Mover

- Ask for a full written quote covering manpower, stairs, dismantling
  and materials, not just "truck from $X"
- Check whether weekend, after-hours or urgent bookings carry a
  surcharge
- Confirm what happens if your building has no lift access
- Read recent reviews, not just the ones featured on the company's own
  site
