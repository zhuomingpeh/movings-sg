Packing is where most damage happens on a move that isn't planned
properly. Here's exactly what's included.

## Packing Materials Provided

- Carton boxes in various sizes
- Bubble wrap for fragile items
- Stretch film for furniture protection
- Wardrobe boxes for clothes on hangers
- Shrink wrap and corner protectors for bulky or delicate furniture

## Full or Partial Packing

- **Full packing**: we pack everything in your home or office
- **Partial packing**: we only pack selected rooms or items, such as
  kitchenware, fragile decor, or electronics

## Fragile and Valuable Items

Glassware, dishes, ceramics, mirrors, paintings, and electronics get
extra padding and custom wrapping. Jewellery, documents and other
valuables are packed only with your instruction.

## Furniture Dismantling and Assembly

Large items like sofas, cabinets and beds are wrapped with stretch film
and padding before loading. Dismantling happens on-site, and reassembly
is included at the destination.

## Labelling

Every box is labelled by room, marked if fragile, and numbered for
inventory on request, so unpacking at the other end doesn't turn into
guesswork.

## Unpacking (Optional)

If you'd rather not unpack yourself, we can unbox everything, dispose of
the packing materials, and place furniture where you want it.

## Cost

Packing and unpacking is priced at SGD 12 per carton to pack and SGD 8
per carton to unpack, with dismantling and assembly at SGD 80-120 per
item. See the [full rate table](/pricing) for how this fits into a
complete move quote.

## FAQ

**Do you provide packing materials?**
Yes: carton boxes, stretch film, bubble wrap, wardrobe boxes and tape.

**Can I pack some items myself?**
Yes, partial packing is available if you'd rather handle some items on
your own.

**How early should I book?**
1-2 weeks in advance is enough to ensure availability and proper
planning.

**How long does packing take?**
A 3-room HDB usually takes 3-5 hours with a full team.

**Is dismantling and reassembly included?**
Yes, for furniture that can't move safely in one piece.
