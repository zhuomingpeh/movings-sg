Most movers stop by 6pm. If you've got an urgent office relocation, a
last-minute home move, or need to clear out before sunrise, we run
late-night and midnight moves across Singapore.

**Before you book:** some condos and commercial buildings restrict moving
past 6-9pm. Check with your management office or MCST and get any
required approval before confirming a slot.

## Why People Move at Midnight

- Office relocations that must happen after business hours
- Urgent lease endings or next-day landlord handovers
- Discreet residential moves for personal reasons
- Post-renovation clean-ups needing furniture shifted
- Short moves between nearby HDB units or landed homes

## Before You Book

1. **Check with your condo or MCST.** Many restrict moving after 5-6pm
   and may require advance booking of service lifts or moving permits.
2. **HDB and landed properties are more flexible.** Late-night moves are
   generally allowed; inform neighbours if you expect noise.
3. **We can help with permits.** If you need help contacting building
   management or submitting vehicle details, ask us.

## Why Book With Us

- Manpower-only or full truck service available at night
- Transparent night pricing, no hidden charges
- Urgent same-day or same-night slots subject to availability

After-hours slots are limited, especially around month-end lease
expiries, Friday nights, weekends, and renovation handover periods.

## FAQ

**Can I move at midnight in a condo?**
Most condos don't allow moving after 5-6pm. Check with your MCST; we can
help with permit submissions.

**Can I move at midnight from an HDB or landed home?**
Yes, generally allowed.

**Do you charge extra for late-night moves?**
Night surcharges are stated upfront, no hidden overtime fees.
