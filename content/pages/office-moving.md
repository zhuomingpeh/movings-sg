Commercial moves need more than a truck and manpower. We plan office,
retail, F&B and warehouse moves around downtime, lift booking, loading
bay access, IT handling and building management rules.

## What We Cover

- Office relocation: workstations, desks, chairs, meeting rooms, cabinets,
  files and pantry items
- Retail, clinic, tuition centre, warehouse and F&B equipment moving
- Furniture dismantling, reassembly, packing, labelling and
  department-by-department placement
- IT and server handling: monitors, CPUs, laptops, printers, phones,
  network racks
- Temporary storage and commercial disposal if the new site isn't ready

## IT, Server and Equipment Handling

List IT equipment separately from general furniture. Computers, monitors,
printers, phones, network equipment and servers need careful packing,
labelling and sequencing. Server shutdown, backup, reconnection and
testing should be coordinated by your IT team; we handle physical
protection and transport.

## Vendor@Gov Capability

For public-sector or procurement-controlled work, we can support
Vendor@Gov-related commercial moving requirements where the buyer's
process allows it. See [vendor-gov](/vendor-gov) for the full scope, or
share your quotation format, documentation requirements and moving
window early so we can plan properly.

## After-Hours and Weekend Moves

Many businesses can't move during operating hours. Subject to manpower,
vehicle availability and building management approval, we plan moves
after office hours, overnight, or on weekends to cut disruption to staff
and customers.

## Lift Booking and Building Management

Before moving day, we confirm service lift slots, loading bay timing,
parking rules, security registration, insurance or permit requirements,
and approved working hours. These access details affect manpower, timing
and cost.

## Our Process

| Stage | Typical timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Scope check | 3-14 days before | Photos, item list, workstation count, access rules, downtime limits |
| Site and access planning | 2-7 days before | Lift booking, loading bay, route, floor protection, permits |
| Packing and labelling | 1-3 days before or on move day | Cartons, department labelling, IT equipment separated |
| Move execution | Move day, after-hours or weekend | Load, transport, unload, place by department |
| Handover | Same day or next working day | Check placement, leftover items, storage or disposal needs |
